## Storybook rules

- **Type.** Two self-hosted open-source fonts in `assets/fonts/` (SIL Open Font License): Fredoka for titles, buttons, tags and canvas text, Nunito for body text. No system-ui on canvas, no other fonts.
- **No tracked uppercase.** No small spaced-out capital "eyebrow" labels and no letter-spaced canvas text. Tags are pill `.card-tag`s in sentence case.
- **Short game labels.** "Level 4 clear!", "Wave 3 of 10", "Unlock levels 5 to 10 · $2.99". Hints are a few words and fit one line (wave hints at most about 50 characters). Prices and what a purchase contains stay exact.
- **Chunky tactile UI.** Pill buttons with a solid darker bottom edge that presses down (green primary, gold for purchases, cream secondary), cream panels with an inked edge, ribbon titles with notched tails, filled tab icons. Colors are tokens at the top of `styles.css`; each level also tints the page (`body[data-theme="depth-…"]`).
- **Scene art.** Painted in light and air with the kit in `scenery.js`: one key light per scene, shapes modelled toward it with gradients and a lit rim, soft shadows, far layers sinking into haze, a final grade and paper grain. No ink outlines on scenery (those belong to the pieces in play); small hand-placed creatures keep soft colored lines and their personality. Seeded randomness so a scene is identical every time, and busy detail kept to the edges and below the danger line so falling flowers read instantly.
- **No AI-generated raster art.** Interface graphics are CSS and inline SVG; scenes and sprites are Canvas paths. `ART-PROVENANCE.md` records this honestly; keep it accurate.

Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/storybook/` (interface), `docs/screenshots/levels/` (levels 1 to 4 and the map), `docs/screenshots/levels-deep/` (levels 5 to 10 and the unlock).

- Finale, aim and danger (`feel.js`): Feel (feel.js plus small hooks in app.js and art.js) handles the camera, game-time scale and the big moments. - **feel.js** is a UMD module with no DOM and no randomness. Feel.create({motion}) returns a small tween machine: request(kind, opts) takes 'finale', 'impact', 'release', 'dip' or 'punch', and step(realDt) updates `out` = {scale, zoom, fx, fy, vignette, busy, phase}. All timings are in Feel.timings. createHitStop() gives at most .09 s per hit and .09 s per rolling second; app.js's jolt() routes its freeze through it. createGovernor() turns frame times into game.quality (0, 1 or 2) and never steps down. predictHit() asks Engine.earliest whether a seed meets a flower within a horizon. - **The finale (app.js, feel block above processEvents).** finaleWatch() predicts the hit on the final flower: the boss on its last ring on a level's last wave, or the last open flower every fifth endless-Rush wave. It requests 'finale' (time to .22, zoom 1.16, dusk vignette), and feelEvent() turns the real bloom into the impact. startSweep() sets bud.flareAt for the gold light and its notes. The result is held (resultAt = Infinity) until the sweep ends and feel is idle, then waits 1.25 s, with a 4 s cap; a tap skips it. - **Danger and aim.** Losing a life writes game.wilts and game.heartBreak and flashes red. aimFeel()/aimLock() click the dial and announce lock-ons. - **art.js** draws the pod's slingshot pose and seed pull-back, the lock-on arrowheads, comet seeds, wilt ghosts, broken hearts and the screen-space veils (drawFeelOverlay). Glows, veils, wilts and streaks are sprites cached at 3x in feelSprites (OffscreenCanvas, then a page canvas, then plain paths in Node), so seed drawing makes no per-frame gradients or shadow blur. - **The camera** is applied once at the top of draw(), so the backdrop zooms too. coordinates() maps touches back with Feel.toBoard. - **Reduced motion** (save.settings.motion false) keeps feel neutral: the finale chord and title still play and the result comes after .4 s. - **Tests and checks.** qa/test-feel.cjs covers the module and plays the finale through app.js; scratch Playwright scripts were used for screenshots and the 4x-throttle trace.
- Super Bloom, tricks, bosses and the wave-6 retry (`rush.js`, `depths.js`): Super Bloom, trick shots, the chain badge, named bosses and the wave-6 checkpoint (rules package). **How crediting works.** rush.js tracks which seed earned each bloom. `this.striker` is the seed whose collision is being resolved. Pending chain items carry {by, from, via, at, depth}, so blooms that come due after a seed is gone still count for it, through `this.due`. - The bloom moment (`art.js` flowers): Bloom package (art.js, qa/test-bloomfx.cjs). A bloom is stateless: `drawUnfurl(ctx, bud, age, options)` is a pure function of the bud, its age since `bloomAt`, and a seed hashed from `bud.id`. - Over .55 s it shows: a one-frame white silhouette; cracks from the struck side (impactAngle + PI); a squash and bubble-pop of the closed bud; casing shards drawn as one path per bud; then the outer and inner layers opening petal by petal from the petal facing `impactAngle` (order 0, 1, -1, 2, -2 ...); the heart pop at .2–.32 s; and a .05 s crossfade into `drawFlower(1)`. - All art comes from 3x sprites in a small LRU atlas (`atlasSprite`, keyed by type and radius) painted with the same `petal()` and `paintFlower` geometry. `spritesFor` warms every sprite a bloom needs on its first frame. - For regular buds (r ≤ 16), each layer's opening is baked lazily into a sheet of frames (`sheetFor` / `sheetFrame`, 6 columns, 2.25x, at most 12 sheets). The frames have the lead petal pointing up and are turned into place by `drawSheet`, so an unfurl is about 4–9 drawImage calls. Bosses go petal by petal. - Sprites are placed with `readBase`/`setFrame`/`stamp`: the transform is read once and each sprite gets its full matrix in one `setTransform`. - `pickUnfurls` gives the full unfurl to the 16 blooms closest to done (6 at quality 2); the rest crossfade over the same .55 s. - `findTremble`/`trembles` pick the one group due at `dangerY` within .8 s. - `drawFlares` adds every finale glow in one 'lighter' pass and all the glints as one star path. - `drawChainLinks` gathers up to 24 pending items that have `from` (12 at quality 1, none at quality 2) and draws them kind by kind (boss streams, sun rays, relay arcs, puff spores), then every head in one additive pass. - Every feature falls back to plain paths when there is no canvas (node), keeps save/restore balanced, and goes still under reducedMotion: a dotted thread for links, a red pip for the tremble, no flash, shards or flares. - To profile, measure on a tiny canvas with CPU throttle; headless software raster exaggerates drawImage cost (about 22 µs per call at 4x).
- Intros, harvests and boss showdowns: **The stage (app.js stage block above "Entering a current"; art.js helpers after drawCallout)** The stage gives depth levels and endless Rush a beginning, a rhythm and a villain. Everything is presentation stored on the game object, never in the save, and the engine never waits for it. Hitboxes never move: only the drawing is offset, which bloom draws from the enter fields. **app.js.** stageFrame(dt) runs once per frame after the score snap. - **New game:** it pushes the 'title' ribbon (x 210, y 210, life 1.2), sets game.stage.introAt, plays 'intro', and tumbles the first wave. - **tumble():** sorts the open buds by x and gives each enterAt / enterDrop 48 / enterDur .4, staggered by min(.035, .35/n). It queues at most 8 'plop' cues at least 40 ms apart, timed to the first touchdown. A wave event also pushes a small 'title'. - **Each frame:** it fires due plops, steps game.harvest on real dt, and lands the boss. It also keeps the boss vine's hp, damage lag (0.1 s hold, then shrinks over 0.4 s) and falling leaves, and removes enter fields once a bud has landed. - **startHarvest() on 'cleared':** turns up to 24 bloomed buds still in full bloom (`BloomArt.spentFlower`), latest first, into orbs. Each arrival calls bumpScore() and plucks. - **bossIntro():** drops the boss from 140 px with a bounce. On landing it plays 'bossLand' and jolts, then shows a name card (kind 'title', boss true) and the vine. **art.js.** - **Callouts:** drawCallout sends kind 'title' to drawTitle (cream ribbon, or a berry/gold boss card), 'trick' to drawStamp (a -6° stamp with a glyph and colour per kind from STAMPS), and 'life' to drawLifeBand. - **drawStage(ctx, state, time, options):** draws the boss vine, the harvest orbs and the score-corner glow, all under an inverse camera transform. It then draws the intro curtain (two cached strips) while introAt is under 0.6 s old and motion is on. - **Sprites:** every piece is painted once by stageSprite(key, w, h, paint) into a 3x OffscreenCanvas, kept in a 64-entry LRU. Text sprites wait for document.fonts.check, and node falls back to live paths. The orbs share one 32 px glow sprite, and the vine leaves are cached per (max, hp). - **Boss moods:** drawBossFace has them. At half health or less: narrowed eyes, heavy brows and gritted teeth. At 20% or less: animated sweat drops and a ±0.04 rad, 6 Hz shiver, also applied to drawBossLeaves. - **Reduced motion:** keeps the titles, card and vine. - Soundtrack and cues (`sound.js`): Audio (sound.js, qa/test-sound.cjs, qa/test-music.cjs). sound.js holds both the cue engine and the soundtrack, synthesized entirely in code with no samples. Soundtrack: - Eleven grooves are defined in SOURCE, TUNES, PULSES and KITS: home for menus and endless Rush, and one per level keyed by Depths level key. Variants inherit their parent's feel via `from`. - Each groove is 16 beats in C major. Chords are written in scale steps through chord([degree, ...extras]), so everything built from them stays diatonic. - score(name, layer) turns a groove into notes. compose() lays them out as small jobs in an OfflineAudioContext. render() builds those jobs in about 2 ms slices, renders mono at 24 kHz (falling back to 22.05k, then 44.1k), folds the 2 s tail back over the loop point, and returns an exact loop buffer. - Loops live in a 12 MB LRU cache. One render runs at a time: the bed first, then the layers in use. - The live mix goes per-layer gain, then the groove gain, then level (menus -6 dB, pause 40%), then a lowpass (900 Hz when paused, muffle, 4200 Hz open), then the music bus (0.2, ducks -4 dB on finale, superBloom and boss), then the existing master chain. Cues go through their own bus into the same master. - All layer sources share one start time, so they stay phase-locked. Changes happen on the next beat with 120 ms ramps, through automation lanes that remember every scheduled point so nothing jumps. - app.js calls BloomSound.music.frame(state) about 10 times a second (and immediately on route, pause, result or visibility changes) with route, groove, heat, threat, superBloom, boss, paused, result, over and hidden. It returns a snapshot for QA. - Heat and threat fall slowly and released layers stay two more beats, so the band builds and thins musically. - Without OfflineAudioContext the music is silently off. The Music setting is absent from the defaults: missing means on. **Fire rate (October 11).** Trevor, after playing the fun pass: "ALMOST PERFECT ... The only thing is is the rate of fire. I was firing as fast as I could click. Now the launcher is a seed pod (all rules in `rush.js`; levels and Meadow Rush share them; `depths.js` only changes three level paces, below):

- The pod holds `POD` = 4 seeds and each shot takes one, no closer than `POD_GAP` = .15 s apart, so a burst of four is quick but never instant.
- Every flower a seed touches or sets off returns `REFUND` = half a seed: a bloom, a ring cracked off, each flower of a crown bunch, a puffcap's chain, a boss hit. A cup that turns a seed away returns nothing; the first time a turning shell turns a seed away it returns `GLANCE` = a quarter seed (hitting a shell's opening is a timing shot, and level 6 starved younger players of seeds without it; only the first time, because every deflection paid the rough masher on level 10 too). Which strikes count is decided by the existing `_credit`, so only seed-earned work feeds the pod.
- An empty slot also regrows on its own, one at a time, in `POD_REGROW` = 6 times the wave's old reload (`regrowTime`: 3.43 s on level 1 wave 1, 2.52 s on level 10, 3.9 s down to 2.7 s in Meadow Rush).
- A tap at most `KEEP` = .15 s before the next shot is allowed is kept and fires the moment it is, with the latest aim (`kept` event), so a quick double tap is never lost. An aimed shot, the finger held at least `HELD` = .2 s (`fire(dx, dy, held)`; `app.js` passes how long the finger was down), is kept up to `AIM_KEEP` = .6 s early, so letting go just before the seed regrows still fires it. A kept tap that cannot fire in time (five seeds went up meanwhile, or the pod ran out) reports `dropped` with its reason instead of vanishing. Any other tap on an empty pod is dropped with a `dry` event.
- Super Bloom fires from the sun instead: a seed every .22 s that costs nothing, while the pod keeps regrowing. Script order: store-config → store → levels → moon → koi → keepsakes → garden → goals → powers → engine → foes → bosses → depths → rush → tutorial → feel → scenery → art → foe-art → meadow → petals → sound → native → app → store-ui → pwa. `sw.js` ASSETS lists every shipped file; bump `VERSION` whenever an asset changes (the Pages build replaces it with a content hash). The native copy step derives its file list from ASSETS.

Every new buzz goes through `haptic(kind)` in `app.js`, never `navigator.vibrate`, or iPhone stays silent.

In levels and Rush the hint bar (`#game-hint`) is hidden, so a tip the player must see goes through `levelTip(text, { again, key, due })` in `app.js`, never only `#game-hint`. It shows the tip as a toast once the toast being read has had its 2.8 s, only while the board is in play, at most every `again` seconds of play per `key` (Infinity: once a run), and asks `due()` again just before it shows. A guardian's turned-away tip is quiet while a move is warned about or it changes mood. Covered in `qa/test-stage.cjs`.

`#toast` is `aria-hidden`, so `#announcement` (polite, atomic) is the one live region, and it is read once the task that wrote it ends: a second `say()` in the same frame or tap would replace the first unheard. So `say()` joins the lines said in one go, each once (a line that holds an earlier one takes its place). A frame, `processEvents()` and a few multi-line taps (planting, a hello, resuming a run, turning the petal) run inside `sayTogether()`; a new handler that says two things (a `toast()` speaks too) wraps itself the same way, or says one line. Opening a level puts away a pop-up left from the screen before (`clearToast()`), and the count after a pause clears the callouts frozen behind it, except a Super Bloom or Lullaby explainer whose effect still runs (its `live` field).

**Who's who (November 7).** How to play has a field guide after Levels: every piece with a rule, every foe and every guardian, grouped by the level they debut in, with a picture, the name and one line on how to beat it (guardians: what to watch for). The list is `GUIDE` near the top of `app.js` (before `readSave`, which uses it), one row per entry in the order a player meets them: `{ id, level, wave, kind, name, line }`. `id` is what `save.met` keeps: a classic piece's own id (with `take`, which piece of its first wave the picture shows), a `foes.js` family (`kind: 'foe'`; the picture is that foe built by `Foes.make` at the middle of a board) or `boss-<kind>` (`kind: 'guardian'`; its name and wave come from `depths.js`). First contact is recorded by `meetBoard()` (whatever is on the board when a level or wave starts, a drop or a foe arrives; a guardian from any unbloomed piece of its fight that carries `bossKind`, so Crystal Heart, who opens as three hearts with her own bud hidden, counts at once) and `meet(['gift'])`, only in the levels (never in Meadow Rush, the tutorial or a preview); a save from before the guide is backfilled from `depths` (a cleared level, or each wave up to the best one reached), and the end of every level run applies the same backfill, so the guide matches what the next load would show. An entry not met yet is a "?" card that only says "Meet it in Level N" (paid levels the same, nothing about buying). The review link (`?alllevels`, and `&bosses`) shows everything. Pictures are drawn lazily: nothing until the section opens, then `guideStep()` from `frame()` paints a few a frame by calling `BloomArt.draw` on a one-wave sandbox `RushGame` holding just that piece (still frame, at most 2x, `options.backdrop: false`), and keeps each canvas; a newly met entry is the only one painted on the next visit. The current's picture is its upstream end on a dark tint of Level 5's water (the pale band does not read on the card's own color); the cup's and the shell's leave out their bunch's crowned bud. A row's framing is data, set only where the plain square left its picture small or faint (measured in real Chromium): `gather` pulls spread-out parts in toward their middle, `crop` cuts the square, and `under` is a backing in the level's own colors (middle, edge), now under the current, the rock, the puffcap, the Ammonite Queen, Wishing Stars and The Starseed; the glowworm is shown curled round its first turn. No framing cuts off a guardian's face and crown or a foe's whole body. `qa/test-guide.cjs` keeps a floor under the framing (a guardian drawn at least .52 of its board size, a piece's own discs covering at least 17% of the frame) and `qa/check-responsive.cjs` under the painted pixels (every piece covers at least 20% of its frame). How to play keeps Who's who open from the last visit only when Levels is the section for this screen (it shuts over Meadow Rush, the gardens and the tutorial), and opened during a level it scrolls that level's group to the top (`guideShown()`). **A new foe needs a row**: add `{ id: '<family>', level, wave, kind: 'foe', name, line }` where it debuts (`line` at most 70 characters, advice that agrees with its `hint` in `foes.js`; `opts` if `Foes.make` needs any); a new guardian needs `{ id: 'boss-<kind>', level, kind: 'guardian', line }`. `qa/test-guide.cjs` fails until every `foes.js` family and every guardian has a row at the level and wave where `depths.js` first shows it, and it checks first contact (every entry live at its debut wave, every guardian at a run that starts on its wave), the save field, the "?" cards, the review link, the lazy, one-time painting within each frame's 6 ms, which section Help opens and the cards' text contrast; `qa/check-responsive.cjs` checks the open guide fits the dialog at every size and at 280 px wide (two columns from 700 px wide, where the help dialog widens to 640 px, with a guardian that would sit alone taking the whole row).

      How to play

      LevelsTen waves
      1. 1

        Ten waves

        Keep the flowers above the line. Clear all ten waves to finish the level and open the next one.

      2. 2

        New pieces

        Rings take more hits. Cups block shots from below. Puffcaps bloom everything near them. Rocks bounce your seeds. Deeper down: currents, turning shells, tunnels, geodes and briars.

      3. 3

        Stars

        Finish with all three lives for ★★★. The last wave of every level is a big bloom.

      4. 4

        Powerups

        Tap one above the board, then fire. Sunburst, Dandelion and Bee Line change your next shot. Lullaby stops the flowers falling for a moment. Shoot a gift bubble to keep the powerup inside.

      Tap the leaf to turn it and bend your shots.

      Meadow RushEndless
      1. 1

        Aim and fire

        Drag to aim, release to fire. Another seed is ready a moment later.

      2. 2

        Split

        Six direct hits charge Split. Tap it while a seed is flying for two more.

      3. 3

        Hold the line

        Gold-ring flowers bloom their neighbors. Three clusters over the line ends the run.

      Tap the leaf to turn it and bend your next shots.

      GardensPuzzles
      1. 1

        Aim and fire

        Drag to aim, let go to fire three seeds. You get three shots per garden.

      2. 2

        Steer in flight

        Hold and drag to pull your seeds toward the flowers.

      3. 3

        Chain it

        Bloom 12 flowers in one shot for two extra seeds.

      Tap a leaf between shots to turn it. Later buds take two or three hits.

      Moon GardenTrials
      1. 1

        Use the gates

        Matching gates carry your seed between them. The aim line shows the exit.

      2. 2

        Five seeds

        One seed per shot, and no steering in flight.

      3. 3

        Turn a leaf

        Tap a leaf between shots to change the route. Fewer shots earn more stars.

      Koi ConservatoryPools
      1. 1

        Read the water

        Currents turn your seed the way they flow. The aim line bends with them.

      2. 2

        Ride a lane

        Currents never change speed, only direction. A longer ride turns further.

      3. 3

        Five seeds

        No steering in flight. Turn a leaf between shots for a new angle.

      Pools 1 and 2 are free. Pools 3 to 8 come with the Koi Conservatory.

HOW TO PLAY

Make every seed count.

Meadow RushENDLESS ARCADE
  1. 01

    Aim. Fire. Keep moving.

    Drag to aim and release one seed. Your next shot is ready after 0.65 seconds.

  2. 02

    Choose when to Split.

    Land six direct hits to charge Split. Tap Split while a seed is flying to add two extra seeds.

  3. 03

    Keep the clusters back.

    Gold-ring relays bloom two nearby buds. Your run ends when three clusters cross the bottom line.

Turn the leaf petal to redirect your shots. It can turn again after two seconds.

GardensTHREE-VOLLEY PUZZLES
  1. 01

    Line up your volley.

    Drag to aim. Release to fire three seeds. You get three volleys to clear the garden.

  2. 02

    Guide them in flight.

    Hold and drag to pull your seeds toward the flowers. You have three seconds of guiding power per volley.

  3. 03

    Keep the chain going.

    Bloom 12 flowers in one volley to earn two bonus seeds. You can earn this bonus once per volley.

Tap a leaf bumper between volleys to turn it. In later gardens, layered buds take two or three hits.

Moon GardenFIVE-SEED TRIALS
  1. 01

    Find a path through the gates.

    Matching gates carry a seed between them. The dotted aim line shows where your seed will emerge.

  2. 02

    Make each seed count.

    You have five single-seed shots. Aim before releasing; moon seeds cannot be steered in flight.

  3. 03

    Turn a leaf. Change the route.

    Tap a leaf bumper between shots to rotate it. Clear the flowers with fewer shots to earn more stars.

Koi ConservatoryCURRENT POOLS
  1. 01

    Read the water.

    Currents gently turn your seed toward the way they flow. The dotted aim line follows the water, so you can see the bend before you shoot.

  2. 02

    Ride a lane.

    A current never speeds a seed up or slows it down. It only changes the heading, and a longer ride turns it further.

  3. 03

    Make each seed count.

    Five single-seed shots with no steering in flight. Turn a leaf between shots, and clear the pool in fewer shots for more stars.

Pools 1 and 2 are free. Pools 3 to 8 come with the full Koi Conservatory, a one-time purchase in the app.

On a keyboard, use arrow keys to aim and Space to shoot.

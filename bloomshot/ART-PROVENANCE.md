# BLOOMSHOT art and audio provenance

October 16 graphics/performance follow-up: the existing code-drawn loaded/tray seed paths and shading are cached and reused during their regrowth pop. Projectile presentation interpolates resolved physics positions, and optional graphics presets reduce rendering resolution and decorative effects. No generated raster art, downloaded illustration or commissioned work was added.

Persistent meadow: meadow.js is AI-assisted procedural Canvas game art, with six organic beds, path, pond, foliage and butterflies, plus existing BloomArt flowers. The six decorations bought with seeds (bench, birdhouse and bluebird, water lilies and frog, beehive and bees, lanterns, apple tree and swing) are drawn the same way, as Canvas paths in meadow.js; their card pictures are rendered from that same code. The six meadow friends that come with them (Biscuit the cat, Pip the bluebird, Hopper the frog, Buzz the bee, Glimmer the firefly and Nutmeg the squirrel) are Canvas paths in meadow.js too, and their Collection portraits and silhouettes are rendered from that code; their sounds are synthesized in sound.js. No downloaded or commissioned art was added. Patch names and progression remain live HTML. Native icons and splash images are exports of the existing flower SVG.

Moon is a free playable chapter with six authored trials and real paired teleport gates. Each trial has five single seeds, no guidance and no automatic extra balls. Newly earned Moon stars reward seeds for the permanent meadow. Koi remains a visual preview with Meadow geometry.

Moon artwork in art.js is AI-assisted procedural Canvas illustration. The detailed spherical moon retains its illustrated craters and maria. The functional gates add cyan/violet inset apertures, carved crescent rims, pearl details, jade vines, matching pair marks and orientation notches. Engine gate-use timestamps drive synchronized entry/exit pulses; aim dots and projectile trails preserve the teleport discontinuity. No new raster generation or downloaded artwork was used for these gates.

The gate chime is synthesized in sound.js through Web Audio: a quiet rising two-note response, debounced for rapid crossings and subject to the existing voice, gain and mute limits. It responds to the same gate event as the visual feedback. Planting uses a separate four-note motif. These are AI-assisted code-generated sounds, not recorded or commissioned performances. Instrumented sound tests verify scheduling and limits; they do not replace listening on actual devices.

## Levels

The ten level scenes (Sunny Meadow, Root Tunnels, Mushroom Grotto, Crystal Caves, Glowworm Lake, Fossil Beds, Ember Hollows, Geode Mine, Briar Vault and Starseed Core) are AI-assisted procedural Canvas illustration in scenery.js. They were repainted on October 10, 2026, still entirely in code. Each scene has one key light (the sun, the burrow's daylight, glowing mushrooms, a work lamp, lava, lanterns, a shaft from the vault, the starseed), and every hill, root, mushroom, crystal, fossil, column, timber and arch is modelled toward it with Canvas gradients, a lit rim and a soft shadow, with no ink outline. Far layers sink into haze, and a final color grade, vignette and paper grain finish each picture. The creatures (the bunny, worm, ants, sleeping mole, bat, snail, cave fish, glowworm, axolotl, mole in a hard hat, fire salamander, mine mouse, cave spider, stone rabbit statue, toad and seed sprites) keep soft colored lines. Everything is placed by hand-picked coordinates and drawn from seeded randomness, so each scene is the same every time. The level map cards show slices of those same scenes, rendered on the device. The garden boards (Meadow, Moon Garden and Koi Pond), the world cards drawn from them and the Garden tab map in meadow.js were repainted the same way and with the same brushes. The board pieces (acorn cups, puffcaps, rocks and crystals, the big bloom with its face and ring of hit marks, fossil shells, geodes and their gems, briar vines with their regrow ring, tunnel holes and the cave water) are Canvas paths in art.js and scenery.js. No raster images were generated, downloaded or commissioned for the levels, and none of this art was made by a human artist. The puffcap, cup, shell, big bloom, geode, briar, falling flowers and rock sounds are synthesized in sound.js like the rest.

The new falling enemies of October 21 (the rolypoly, the Peekaboo Mole and his molehills, the fibcap, the Drippy Inkcap and its ink drops, the Chroma Crystal with its color glyphs, the Echo Bat, the Moon Jelly with its vents and glowdrops, and the glowworm) and everything the guardians' new attacks put on the board (Old Sunny's sun portals and snooze Z, Rootknot's holes, lobs, root carousel, knot and snare, Mother Morel's dancing partners, fake caps and ink cloud, Crystal Heart's mirror blades and shards, and Lantern Lily's moon gate, lily pads, glowworm lantern and tide) are Canvas paths in foe-art.js, painted once into the same sprite atlas as the rest and drawn with art.js's brushes. They were written as code with AI assistance, like the other board pieces; no raster image was generated, downloaded or commissioned, and none was drawn by a human artist. Their sounds (thumps, pops, sneezes, drips, chimes and swoops) are synthesized in sound.js from the same instruments.

The four powerups (Sunburst's smiling sun, Dandelion's seed clock, Bee Line's bee and Lullaby's sleepy moon) and the gift bubble with its ribbon bow are AI-assisted Canvas paths in art.js, drawn in the same flat, ink-edged style. The tray buttons and the Powerups shelf show pictures rendered on the device from that same code. Their sounds (arming, each powerup, the sunburst, the bee, the gift arriving and being caught) are synthesized in sound.js. No raster images were generated, downloaded or commissioned for them.

The petal shower over a won result (petals, leaves and small blossoms with an inked edge and a center crease) is drawn with AI-assisted Canvas paths in petals.js, and the star pops, banner and new-level greeting are CSS animations. No raster images were used for them.

The sound effects were rebuilt on October 9, still entirely synthesized in sound.js with Web Audio: kalimba, marimba, wood-block, chime, water-drop and soft-chord voices made from a few sine partials each, and one shared room echo whose impulse is generated in code from seeded noise. No recorded, downloaded or commissioned audio is used, and the sounds were checked by measurement in a browser, not on a phone speaker.

## Interface

The interface is AI-assisted HTML, CSS and inline SVG. The buttons, panels, ribbon titles, tab icons and the padlock (assets/ui/lock.svg) are vector shapes written in code, not generated images. The game no longer uses any AI-generated raster art: the two image-generator ornaments it used to carry (botanical-header.png behind the score and split-leaf.png behind the Split button) were removed in the October 2026 redesign.

Typefaces: Fredoka for titles and buttons, Nunito for body text. Both are open-source fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted in assets/fonts/ with their license texts, so the game requests no fonts from other servers.

Color pass (October 10, 2026): the forget-me-not and poppy flowers, the colored bud faces, the scenes' color pools and vibrance grade, the praise lettering, the danger-line glow and the colored aim line are all drawn in code in art.js, scenery.js and styles.css, the same way as the rest. No raster images were generated, downloaded or commissioned.

Fun pass (October 10, 2026): the finale camera and gold sweep, the petal-by-petal unfurl and its frame sheets, the bosses' faces, crowns, name cards and health vines, the trick stamps, the sun fan and chain badge, the intro curtains, the harvest orbs and the wilted ghosts are all drawn in code (art.js, app.js, feel.js), and the soundtrack and every new cue are synthesized in sound.js. No raster images or audio samples were generated, downloaded or commissioned.

Fire rate (October 11, 2026): the seed pod's empty cup and refill ring, the seed tray beside it, the coral shake of an empty tap, the hollow aim line shown while the pod refills and the little seeds of light that fly back to the tray are Canvas paths in art.js, and the pod's refill and empty sounds are synthesized in sound.js. No raster images or audio samples were generated, downloaded or commissioned.

Named boss reconstruction (October 13, 2026): the recovered design reference informed ten distinct AI-assisted Canvas illustrations in art.js: Old Sunny's sunflower and snore bubble, Rootknot's knotted wood and flower crown, Mother Morel's pitted cap and movable bonnet, Crystal Heart's pink facets and beating core, Lantern Lily's lily pad and glowworm lantern, the Ammonite Queen's coiled shell, monocle and pearl tiara, Ember Rose's flame petals and castanet leaves, the Geode King's mine cart, crust and amethyst heart, Thornmother's fuchsia bell and healing vines, and the Starseed's star, seed swirl, dome and five satellites. Static paths are cached on the device, while poses and defensive overlays follow bosses.js modes. Breakable bubbles, hearts, lanterns, crust plates and satellite stars are drawn at the real engine part positions and radii; spent or hidden parts do not become extra targets. Warning footprints come from the controller's actual pending move. The artwork is original code reconstruction based on the recovered written reference, not a claim that the missing original boss source or images were recovered. No raster-generation tool, downloaded artwork or commissioned performance was used.

Scene life (October 14, 2026): each level now has two occasional visitors drawn with AI-assisted Canvas paths in art.js and scheduled by a deterministic visual clock in scenery.js. Sunny Meadow has a butterfly and bee; Root Tunnels has a briefly twitching worm and a peeking gopher; Mushroom Grotto has a moth and snail; Crystal Caves has a bat and crystal glint; Glowworm Lake has a glowworm and minnow; Fossil Beds has a beetle and peeking gecko; Ember Hollows has a salamander and drifting ember; Geode Mine has a hard-hat mouse and glint; Briar Vault has a small descending spider and firefly; Starseed Core has a seed sprite and star mote. Wing, tail and blink layers use a separate bounded sprite cache; the overlay does not repaint the static scenes each frame or consume gameplay randomness. Reduced motion removes the visitor layer completely. The labelled QA atlas and silent motion study render these same production Canvas APIs; they are review evidence, not store screenshots or actual-device performance claims. The ignited escorts in Ember Rose's encounter have a cached flame overlay only while their real bossFlameUntil flag is active. No photo, image-generator output, downloaded raster artwork or audio sample was used.

# Bloomshot private playtest, October 15

This follows Trevor's desktop feedback that Sunny looked passive, Level 2's ordinary waves were too difficult, and free powerups were too rare. Trevor also requested a unique change to the falling flowers for every boss. No iPhone play or installation has yet been verified. The private destination is shared with Trevor in the project chat and the local QR handoff, outside these public notes.

## What changed

Sunny previously launched a slow cluster near the ceiling; its trip to the danger line could outlast the whole fight. Waking him also delayed the attack. His first hostile volley now has a 1.3-second tell, two one-hit gold sprouts, and five seconds to stop them. Ignoring the pair costs one life in total; ordinary aimed shots can stop both. Popping the bubble still rewards a six-second double-hit opening. Sunny remains at 14 health.

Level 2's ordinary pace is 1.4 rather than 1.55, about 10% slower. Its rocks and flower layouts remain intact. Rootknot's health and base movement are unchanged.

Regular free gift bubbles arrive early in campaign waves 2, 4, 6 and 8, and every even wave of endless Rush. They offer all four powers before repeating, from a random starting type, and drift more slowly than before. A blocked entrance retries rather than losing the drop. Catching a bubble keeps its power; missing it loses no life and never holds up a wave. Boss waves retain their separate last-life Lullaby gift.

All four powerup controls now share one bottom row directly above Split, including on short portrait screens. Each retains a 44-pixel touch target, count and accessible name. Following Trevor's next screenshot, the bottom message pill is hidden during levels and Rush; its former band extends the board downward. The danger line moves about 33 pixels lower on the checked phones, and the pod about 36–37 pixels lower, with identical powerup/action bounds. Physics coordinates and fall timing are unchanged. Attack warnings still appear on the board; accessible announcements still name both the attack and its flower effect.

The tutorial's cards moved high to keep the bottom buttons uncovered. Actual tutorial input completed all seven lessons and twelve prompts at 360×640, 390×844 and 820×1180, including all four power activations. Browser geometry checks cover every campaign level, Moon and Koi at those sizes, and touch cancellation/resizing at 390×664 and 390×844. These are desktop Chromium checks, not Safari or physical-device evidence.

Level 3's regular flowers fall about 7% slower (pace 1.32 instead of 1.42), with fewer cups in waves 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 9. The first cup wave has eight cupped flowers across its opening and reinforcement instead of eighteen. Fully cupped crowns arrive in wave 5, after practice with exposed crowns and puffcaps. Flower counts, health and Mother Morel's boss health/base movement remain intact.

Trevor approved revised USD powerup targets on October 15: one for $0.29, five of a chosen kind for $0.49, twenty mixed (five of each) for $1.00. The twenty bag uses a new product id, `bloomshot.pack.bag20`; the retired twelve bag's saved grants retain their original contents. Every product remains unavailable until the actual store offer and a real phone sandbox purchase are verified. Other purchase prices are unchanged.

## Every guardian changes the flowers

The existing boss move and its flower spell share a 1.3-second warning. Marked flowers carry a small themed cue; the board banner names the attack and the accessible announcement includes the flower change. At most three ordinary flowers are affected at once, usually two. Gifts, bosses, boss parts, puffcaps, geodes and loose gems are excluded. The final 100 pixels above the danger line are protected from these transformations. Temporary effects end cleanly on expiry, breach, defeat or wave changes.

| Boss | Falling-flower effect |
| --- | --- |
| Old Sunny | Swells two buds to 125% radius, making larger real targets alongside his sprout volley. |
| Rootknot | Shrinks two buds to 70% radius, with an eight-pixel minimum. |
| Mother Morel | Veils two flower fills to 40% opacity, keeping their outlines, crowns and hit boundary visible. |
| Crystal Heart | Makes two flowers sway sideways through bounded actual movement. |
| Lantern Lily | Briefly lifts two flowers before their normal descent resumes. |
| Ammonite Queen | Wraps two buds in temporary turning shells with real shootable openings. |
| Ember Rose | Ignites at most three marked flowers, briefly hurrying their descent. |
| Geode King | Adds one temporary real hit ring, capped at three HP, once per bud; expiry never heals damage. |
| Thornmother | Turns one clean group into a temporary thorn patch with a visible two-second regrowth clock. |
| The Starseed | Makes opaque echo copies at marked clear destinations: at most two live copies and four created per encounter. They cannot copy themselves and disappear before the final reaction margin. |

The larger/smaller drawings use the actual collision radius. Lifting and swaying use actual positions. Lullaby preserves the warning/effect timing while stopping the harmful motion. Reduced motion retains static readable cues. One initial Thornmother escort group remains ordinary so her first conversion has a visible target.

## Evidence scope

The October 14 corpus, screenshots and preview retain their original source fingerprints. They are historical evidence for that earlier build. Retake store media from the approved final version, rather than treating those drafts as captures of the new spells. Source-specific current results are recorded in bloomshot/qa/playtest-feedback-results.json; the deployed artifact is identified separately by bloomshot/WEB-RELEASE.json.

The paired Level 2 pace comparison used 16 seeds for each of two artificial profiles, without gifts or inventory powers. The existing average profile cleared 16/16 before and after; a slower, less precise sensitivity profile cleared 10/16 before and 15/16 after. All failures in those pairs happened before reaching Rootknot, and neither pair timed out. This comparison preceded the later boss-flower spells and establishes an effect on the ordinary-wave adjustment, not current whole-campaign human difficulty.

Real-device behavior, human difficulty and retention, native signing, sandbox purchases and commercial results remain unverified. All products remain disabled, and the private site's audience is preserved.

The paired Level 3 comparison used the same 16 seeds for each artificial profile, without gifts or inventory powers, on the current boss mechanics. Average clears increased from 13/16 to 16/16; slower-profile clears increased from 5/16 to 12/16. Neither pair timed out. This small sensitivity comparison supports easing the cup introduction; Trevor's playtest determines whether it feels right.

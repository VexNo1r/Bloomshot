# Bloomshot private playtest, October 14

Historical snapshot: this page and its source fingerprints describe the October 14 build. The later Sunny attack, Level 2 easing, regular free gifts and ten flower-changing boss effects are documented in PLAYTEST-2026-10-15.md. The earlier corpus and captures are not evidence for those later mechanics.

This pass prioritizes the game and a thorough bug audit. It is not a public release or a claim that the game has been tested on an iPhone. The deployed version is recorded separately in `bloomshot/WEB-RELEASE.json`.

## What to play

Trevor has the owner-private playtest address in the project chat. Use its regular game, append ?alllevels for review, or append ?alllevels&bosses to start selected levels directly at wave ten. Review links use a separate save and do not unlock native purchases. Regular progression and the level-five taste remain intact. Private destinations are omitted from these public notes.

Trevor should try the first two bosses again, then sample Crystal Heart, Lantern Lily, Ammonite Queen, Geode King and the Starseed. Check whether guards and openings make sense before the move lands; whether careful shots replenish the pod more reliably than repeated loose tapping; and whether background visitors stay peripheral. Check sound, pause/resume, the tutorial, Moon and Koi, compact-screen controls and Safari's home-screen/offline behavior on the actual iPhone. Report the device/browser and any confusing, unfair or slow moments.

## Changes

- Ten distinct named guardians with actual moving parts, readable warnings and level-specific abilities. Introductory boss health was strengthened after Trevor's first-pair feedback; later encounters were audited rather than uniformly inflated.
- Timely damaging contacts earn pod refills, once per flower per flight. Old ricochets still bloom and score; repeated glances cannot farm seeds. Four opening seeds, burst spacing, collision sizes and normal regrowth remain intact.
- Painted scenes fill the viewport. Controls respect safe areas, and short portrait phones use edge columns to retain a larger board. Subtle deterministic visitors have long pauses, stay peripheral and disappear with reduced motion.
- The six deeper-level previews use the actual painted levels. Launched ordinary seeds now match the shaded almonds in the pod; existing falling buds, launcher and gift bubbles were retained after inspection.
- Boss fixes include shootable revived plates/stars, Crystal's hidden-body breach/reset, boss-part armor during Super Bloom, exact once-per-boss powered damage through proxies, honest finale prediction and a reaction margin for newly released gems.
- Save/purchase fixes include full-pack capacity before payment, durable pending receipts, recovery after uncertain responses, no paid delivery into a QA save, and no acknowledgement before native backup arbitration. All products remain disabled. Private builds hide Share while no public destination exists.
- The offline policy uses Trevor's October 14 date and approved support email, generated from the same source as the public build's policy.

They use seeded aim/reaction error, visible targets and observed motion, earned Split and Super Bloom, and no powerups or purchases. These profile labels are software settings, not measured human skill groups.

*Fresh fights start on wave ten with three lives and a new pod. Successful-fight times exclude the two failures, which remain included in clear counts. Both failures ran out of lives rather than stalling.

Average-profile campaign pressure S rises from 0.023 at Level 1 to 1.305 at Level 4; all later levels exceed Level 4, and Level 10 is highest at 3.391. S is lives lost on a clear, or three plus the waves remaining on a loss. The sequence is not strictly increasing: Level 9's 2.563 is below Level 8's 2.867, and individual bosses differ in length. Careful aiming can clear every level without bought help. This does not establish human difficulty, retention or commercial success; the iPhone playtest decides whether the opening bosses feel right.

The measurement commands, profile definitions and disjoint validation-seed rules are in `bloomshot/qa/MEASUREMENTS.md`. Earlier partial runs that observed source changes are excluded from this evidence.

A further 320 disjoint validation runs completed with the same frozen sources and zero timeouts. Clears by level were 32, 32, 29, 23, 28, 26, 19, 28, 14 and 18 out of 32. These smaller samples do not confirm every later level being harder than Level 4: Levels 5, 6 and 8 had lower observed pressure than Level 4. Keep that uncertainty visible and compare Levels 4–6 by hand. No tuning was performed after consuming these validation seeds. The compact report with configurations, intervals and source hashes is `bloomshot/qa/campaign-measurement-results.json`.

The local promotional preview decoded at 27.9872 seconds with original synthesized sound and music. Full source fingerprints, scopes and measurements are in `bloomshot/qa/final-audit-results.json`.

The owner-private Site was refreshed successfully; `bloomshot/WEB-RELEASE.json` records its exact Site source, version and deployment IDs. The audience was preserved. Review progress stays separate from the regular save. If an older build appears, use Settings → Restart and update.

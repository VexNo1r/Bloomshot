## Help with Bloomshot

For help or feedback, email **hello@harborlineleadrecovery.com**.

Please include the app version, phone model, iOS/Android version and the level or screen where the problem occurred. Describe what you expected and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove personal information first. Do not send passwords, identity documents, payment-card details or private keys.

### Progress and saves

Bloomshot keeps game progress on your device. The native app also keeps a local Preferences backup. The website and installed app have separate saves, and the game has no cross-device player account. Deleting the app or clearing data can remove progress. Operating-system backups may restore data depending on your settings; restoration is not guaranteed. Unfinished runs restart after a reload.

### Purchases

Purchases are unavailable in the first test build. When purchases are enabled, the store shows their exact contents and price before confirmation. Restore purchases in Settings restores eligible permanent unlocks using the same store account. Spent powerups and local progress are separate from permanent purchases. Contact support with the platform, product and approximate purchase date for delivery problems; do not send payment-card information.

### Privacy

Bloomshot has no player login, ads, chat, advertising tracking or gameplay analytics. The public privacy policy describes local saves, hosting and purchase-service data when purchases are enabled. The release support page must link to that verified public policy before submission.

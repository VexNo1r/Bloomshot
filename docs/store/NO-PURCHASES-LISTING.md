## Description to paste

Aim a seed, bounce it off the walls and watch the flowers bloom. Bloomshot is a botanical arcade game with four free campaign levels, endless Meadow Rush and a meadow you can grow at your own pace.

Drag to aim and release to fire. Look for crowned flowers to bloom a whole bunch, bank shots around obstacles, and keep the falling clusters above the danger line. Each campaign level ends with its own guardian and warned special moves.

Four campaign levels
Sunny Meadow introduces your seed pod and bank shots. Root Tunnels adds rocks to bounce around. Mushroom Grotto brings cupped flowers and puffcaps. Crystal Caves combines the mechanics with moving crystals. After clearing level 4, try the first three waves of Glowworm Lake.

Free powerup gifts
Catch gift bubbles during regular even waves. Sunburst blooms nearby flowers, Dandelion fires three seeds, Bee Line pierces guards, and Lullaby stops the flowers falling for six seconds. Choose a power with the four buttons above Split.

More ways to play
Meadow Rush offers endless falling waves. Garden has eighteen three-volley puzzles. Moon Garden has six trials with paired gates. The Koi Conservatory's first two pools introduce currents that bend your shots. A daily Garden layout offers another way to earn seeds.

Grow your meadow
Earn seeds from completed games and new stars, then plant six permanent flower patches and watch them grow. Meadow growth does not change shooting difficulty.

Free to download. No ads or subscriptions. Gameplay works offline and needs no player account. Purchases are unavailable in this version. Your progress stays on your device; deleting the app or its data can remove it.
